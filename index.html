🙏 Guidelines Before Submission

  • All 108 offerings are traditionally prepared following strict satvik principles — freshly made in cleaned sacred vessels, devoid of onions or garlic, cooked with pure ghee and edible devotion, and offered with heartfelt prayer.
  • Any quantity is not applicable — it is entirely based on the feasibility of the resident.
  • Each Prasadam item can be prepared/submitted by only one resident. Once claimed, it will be shown as taken (with the flat number) and locked for others.
  • If a resident wishes to contribute more than one item, they must submit separate entries — one submission per item, each with a different Prasadam selected.
  • Please deliver the prepared Prasadam in time for the Evening Pooja on 24th September, 6:00 PM.
